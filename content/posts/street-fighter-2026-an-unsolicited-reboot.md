I liked the new Street Fighter movie. I don't think it's good (actually, I think it's a really bad movie), but I think it's an entertaining mess. It's like eating raw cookie dough, sweet, but not good for you and definitely not a good dessert. This is the start of a new series I'll be doing for every piece of entertainment I watch to describe how I'd fix the flaws in the plot, especially things like Street Fighter that seem like they have a lot of potential but fall flat with execution.

So to start, I'm just going to summarize what exactly I remember from the movie last night:

I missed a little bit of the beginning of the movie, but basically M. Bison (the antagonist of the movie) decides to host a "World Fighting Tournament" to extract the power of the MIA Ryu (the co-protagonist along with his estranged best friend Ken). Chun-Li is spying on M. Bison, recruits everyone for the tournament (including finding Ryu and trying to convince him). Ken joins the tournament, Ryu joins after some shenanigans, everyone joins. There's a really unnecesary bar fight designed to introduce 50 Cent's character. The bar fight is also used to get Ryu arrested by M. Bison so he can start extracting his power (which he does after Ryu refuses to help him and uses his evil power to fight Jason Mamoa's character). Ken beats up 50 Cent's character after locking in (in the tournament), beats up the Indian guy too, then after getting convinced by Chun-Li decides to help save Ryu. They break him out of Prison, but get caught, but then Jason Mamoa's character decides to save Ryu, gives him a speech and all. Chun-Li reveals she's a spy to M. Bison, beats up his goons, gets beat up by M. Bison who has Ryu's power. At the same time, Ryu reveals to Ken that Ken's insecurities about being worse than Ryu are based on a lie, because Ryu burned Ken's invite to the first rouanment, which led to Ryu beating Ken as a student, etc. etc, Ryu and Ken fight, Ryu beats Ken's ass and then goes to fight Akaza, who respawns in the tournament, Chun-Li brings Ken to the fighting ground to fight Akaza, but Ryu becomes evil and has already beat Akaza, Ken and Ryu then fight, Ken hits Ryu with a red blast and knocks him out and then they sing him a song and he's not evil anymore. Finally, M. Bison steals Akaza's body and takes his powers, seting up the sequel
