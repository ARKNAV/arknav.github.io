I'm starting this blog for a simple reason: I learn faster when I write things down, and ideas get sharper when they're public.

Most of my work lives in code, notebooks, and half-finished drafts. This is where I'll turn the interesting bits into short, finished essays — what I built, what broke, and what I'd do differently next time.

## What to expect

- **Build notes** — short write-ups from projects in game analytics, RL, and embodied AI.
- **Paper notes** — things I read for coursework (ML, NLP, Game AI) distilled into plain language.
- **Small tutorials** — anything I wish someone had explained to me more clearly.

## How this blog works

Posts are just Markdown files in `content/posts/`. Pushing one to `main` redeploys the site, so there's no CMS to babysit and no database to pay for.

```md
---
title: My new post
date: 2026-10-01
category: Notes
excerpt: One honest sentence about what this post covers.
tags: [ai, notes]
---

Write the post here. Headings, lists, quotes, and code all work.
```

If you're reading this early, there's not much here yet — that's the point. Check back soon, or follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/arknav).
